RESEARCH

Research directions

I seek transferable research ability across systems engineering, visual algorithms, and data-driven modeling, with real-world problems as the starting point.

01

Robotics &
Embodied Intelligence

Robotic system integration, manipulation, hand-eye coordination, motion planning, and multi-module collaboration. The foundation comes from RoboCup, robotic-arm, and robot-dog grasping projects.

02

Computer Vision &
Medical AI

Object detection, keypoint estimation, image segmentation, and vision systems for AI-assisted diagnosis. Representative work includes Open-Eye, EyeMoveAI, and intelligent gauge reading.

03

Machine Learning
for Environmental Systems

During the master's stage, I am exploring multi-source environmental-data modeling, intelligent sensing, and decision support. This is an open research direction, not a premature commitment.

Research evidence

Publications and research: a first-author manuscript on an industrial gauge-reading cascade method is under submission; a lightweight YOLOv8 road-crack detection paper has been published with second authorship.

Training: sustained undergraduate research training through the Big Data and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory; graduate training now expands this foundation toward environmental applications.
