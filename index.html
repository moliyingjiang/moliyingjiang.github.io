M.Sc. Student · Environmental Science & Engineering

I am a master's student in Environmental Science and Engineering at Guangxi Normal University. I studied Internet of Things Engineering at Taiyuan University, working on robotics, robotic manipulation, computer vision, and artificial intelligence.

My current research interests connect machine learning with environmental science, including prediction and interpretation for drinking-water treatment. This site follows my academic journey, from robotics and vision to environmental applications.
