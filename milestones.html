Academic trajectory

A chronological record of study, research, competitions, and transitions since my undergraduate enrollment in 2021. Only verifiable milestones are included.

UNDERGRADUATE · TAIYUAN UNIVERSITY · 2021 — 2025

  1. 2021.10

    Enrolled in Internet of Things Engineering

    Joined the Big Data and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory through an assessment and began research-oriented training through group meetings, presentations, and projects.

  2. 2022

    Medical AI and innovation-training projects

    Participated in an intelligent palm-diagnosis research project; the related work received national-level undergraduate innovation-training support.

  3. 2023

    Medical AI and robotics results emerged

    The ocular-motility AI-assisted diagnosis project received national innovation-training support. Participated in national robotics and computer-design competitions with awarded results.

  4. 2024

    Focused on hand-eye coordination and vision algorithms

    Developed a general robotic hand-eye coordination and grasping system; received a national first prize in the China University Intelligent Robot Creative Competition and a national second prize in the China Robot Competition. A road-crack detection paper was published as second author.

  5. 2025

    Completed undergraduate study and thesis

    The graduation project, “Target Grasping with a Hand-Eye Coordinated Robot Dog,” received Outstanding Graduation Project and Outstanding Graduation Thesis honors.

UNDERGRADUATE STUDY COMPLETED · 2025

GRADUATE · GUANGXI NORMAL UNIVERSITY · 2026 — PRESENT

  1. 2026

    Entered the M.Sc. program in Environmental Science and Engineering

    Began graduate training at Guangxi Normal University and started exploring the intersection of machine learning and environmental science.

  2. 2026.03

    Industrial gauge-reading research continued

    Undergraduate work on a large-and-small model cascade method for industrial gauge reading developed into a first-author manuscript under submission, according to the current CV.

  3. NOW

    Building a reproducible research record

    Graduate-stage papers, projects, datasets, and code will be added only when they reach a publicly verifiable stage.
